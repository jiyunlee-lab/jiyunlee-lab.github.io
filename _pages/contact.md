## Prospective Students

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students interested in joining SHAPE Lab. Our current research focuses on dynamic wildfire risk assessment using earth observation imagery and deep learning, as well as citizen science and engagement in wildfire resilience planning.

Ideal candidates have experience in machine learning (particularly image classification and segmentation), probabilistic/statistical modeling, and game-theoretic models, along with a strong background in computer programming. Candidates from other departments (e.g., Industrial Engineering, Computer Science, Geosciences) are also welcome to apply.

If you are interested in joining the lab, please reach out by email with a brief description of your background and research interests.
